Acoustic Vehicle Distance Detection
for Pedestrian Safety

A multi-class audio classification system that estimates vehicle proximity from acoustic features, with safety-oriented threshold tuning to maximize detection of approaching vehicles in the danger zone.

⚠️ Why Class 1 matters most

A missed detection in Class 1 (danger zone) could cost a pedestrian their life. The threshold tuning strategy deliberately biases the model toward predicting Class 1 whenever there is meaningful probability — accepting more false alarms in exchange for fewer missed dangers.

📊 Interpretation

The ~9% accuracy drop from validation to the unseen test environment is expected and reflects the domain shift between recording conditions. Despite this, XGBoost maintains a strong Class 1 recall (78.4%) even on unseen data, detecting 69 out of 88 dangerous events. This demonstrates reasonable real-world generalization while highlighting the need for environment-robust features in future iterations.

04

Methodology

End-to-end pipeline from raw audio to safety-oriented classification.

Feature Extraction
132 acoustic features per sample: MFCC + Delta + Delta2, spectral features, and Doppler-inspired features.
Preprocessing
Peak normalization, silence trimming (top-db=20), and StandardScaler standardization.
Model Training
Three classifiers: XGBoost (gradient boosting), SVM (RBF kernel), and PyTorch MLP (3-layer).
Threshold Tuning
Safety-oriented: if P(Class 1) > threshold, force Class 1. Target ≥90% recall on danger class.
Evaluation
Validation, file-level split (no leakage), ablation study, ROC/AUC, learning curve, and unseen test.

Feature Groups (132 total)

🎵
MFCC + Delta + Delta2
120 features
Mel-frequency cepstral coefficients and their first/second-order temporal derivatives. Capture timbral characteristics of vehicle sounds.
📊
Spectral Features
10 features
Centroid, bandwidth, rolloff, zero-crossing rate, and RMS energy — each with mean and standard deviation.
🚗
Doppler-Inspired
2 features
Spectral centroid slope over time and centroid diff std. Capture the frequency shift of approaching vehicles.
